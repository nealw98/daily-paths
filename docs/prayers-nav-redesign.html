that You should have all of me,

What's Different
1. Teal header. Same gradient as Today and Journal. "Prayers" in Cormorant Garamond italic. The page immediately feels like it belongs.

2. Prayer cards have an icon. The flame icon (matching the Prayers nav icon) sits to the left of each prayer name in a tinted square. Gives visual weight without being decorative.

3. Expanded text is formatted for reading. Centered, in Cormorant Garamond italic at 18px, with line breaks for natural breathing rhythm. A thin divider separates the title from the text. This should feel like reading from a prayer book, not a web page.

4. Personal Prayer Notes is elevated. Separate section at the bottom with its own icon (pen, in warm brown). The edit icon invites interaction. The placeholder text is warm and inviting.

5. Custom bottom nav icons. Open book with ribbon (Today), feather (Journal), flame (Prayers), stacked stones (More). They share the same organic line weight and style as the journal entry icons. No generic icon pack feel — these are Daily Paths icons.

6. The flame connects Prayers to its identity. It appears as the nav icon, the prayer card icon, and could become the section's visual signature. A flame is universal enough for prayer/meditation without being tied to any specific tradition.
